North facing house plan: entrance, rooms and Vastu

A north facing plot has its road on the north side. The front of the house gets even, glare-free daylight all day with little direct sun, which makes it ideal for large front windows and a bright living room.

Room placement for a north facing house

  • Main entrance: on the north side, towards the north-east.
  • Living room: north or north-east, at the front.
  • Kitchen: south-east, or north-west as the alternative.
  • Master bedroom: south-west, at the rear.
  • Pooja room: north-east.
  • Staircase: south or west, rising clockwise.
  • Toilets: west, north-west or south; not in the north-east.
  • Car parking: north-west or north-east at the front, planned so it does not block the entrance.

Light and climate

North light is the most usable daylight there is: bright, steady and without glare. Put the rooms you use most during the day at the front and give them generous windows. The rear (south) and west walls take the strongest sun, so shade them with deep chajjas, a verandah or a jaali.

Common mistakes

Parking that blocks the north-east. On narrow north facing plots the car porch often swallows the best corner. Shifting it to the north-west usually frees the entrance and living room.

Small front windows. North-facing glass gets little direct sun, so it is the one side where large windows rarely overheat the room.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is a north facing house good as per Vastu?

Yes. North facing plots are considered auspicious in Vastu, with the entrance placed towards the north-east part of the north side.

Where should the master bedroom be in a north facing house?

South-west, which also places it at the quieter rear of the plot.

Where should the staircase be in a north facing house?

In the south or west part of the house, ideally rising clockwise.
