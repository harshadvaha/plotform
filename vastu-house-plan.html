Vastu house plan: where each room usually goes

Vastu Shastra divides a plot into directional zones and assigns each kind of room to the zone that suits it. Most Indian families want at least the key placements followed. This guide shows the conventional layout and how to fit it to a real plot.

Room by room

  • Main entrance: north, east or north-east are preferred. On west and south facing plots, specific parts of the front edge are recommended over others.
  • Pooja room: north-east, the cleanest and lightest corner.
  • Living room: north, east or north-east.
  • Kitchen: south-east (the fire zone); north-west is the accepted alternative.
  • Master bedroom: south-west.
  • Children's and guest bedrooms: west, north-west or south.
  • Staircase: south, west or south-west, rising clockwise.
  • Toilets: north-west or west; never north-east or the centre.
  • Centre (Brahmasthan): open, uncluttered and free of columns where possible.
  • Water: underground sump in the north-east; overhead tank over the south-west.

Where Vastu and good design agree

Much of Vastu lines up with plain climate sense. The north-east gets soft morning light, so it suits a pooja room or living room. The south-west is the hottest corner in the afternoon, so heavy walls, stores and the overhead tank there help shield the rest of the house. An open centre brings light and air into the middle of the plan.

When the plot does not cooperate

Narrow, irregular or small plots rarely allow every placement. When we plan a Vastu house we agree priorities with you first, usually the entrance, kitchen, master bedroom and toilets, and then solve the rest for light, ventilation and space. A plan that follows Vastu on paper but leaves rooms dark and airless is not a good plan.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can every house plan be 100% Vastu compliant?

Rarely, especially on small or irregular plots. Most families prioritise the entrance, kitchen, master bedroom and toilet positions.

Which facing is best as per Vastu?

North and east facing plots are traditionally preferred, but west and south facing plots can be planned well with the right entrance position and room placement.

Does Plotform design Vastu-based house plans?
