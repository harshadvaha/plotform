30x50 house plan: a 3BHK on 1500 sq ft

A 30×50 plot gives you an extra ten feet of depth over a 30×40, and that depth is what makes a 3BHK on a single floor realistic. The challenge shifts from fitting rooms in to keeping a long, narrow house bright and airy through the middle.

What fits on a 30×50 plot

1500 sq ft of plot area typically supports one of these, once setbacks and floor area limits are applied:

  • Single floor 3BHK: three bedrooms, living, dining, kitchen with utility, and two or three bathrooms, often with a car porch at the front.
  • Ground + 1 for a joint family: a full 2BHK on each floor, or a shared living floor below and all bedrooms above.
  • Home plus rental: owner's unit on one floor and an independent unit with a separate stair.

Planning a long, narrow house

Arrange rooms front to back by privacy. Parking and living at the road end, dining and kitchen in the middle, bedrooms at the rear where it is quiet. A single passage down one side links them without cutting through rooms.

Break the depth with light. Fifty feet is too deep for side windows alone when neighbours build up close. A small open court, a skylight over the stair or a double-height dining space brings daylight and cross-ventilation to the middle of the house.

Put the stair where it serves both floors. Near the middle of one side, the stair reaches the upper bedrooms without eating into the living room, and can be closed off later if you rent the upper floor.

Facing and Vastu on a 30×50 site

The usual Vastu placements hold here: entrance in the north, east or north-east, kitchen in the south-east, master bedroom in the south-west, pooja in the north-east and the centre kept open. On a long plot the south-west master bedroom often ends up at the rear, which also makes it the quietest room.

Climate matters as much as direction. Whichever way the plot faces, the west wall takes the hottest afternoon sun, so keep bedroom windows on that side small and shaded.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can I fit a 3BHK on a 30×50 plot?

Usually yes, on a single floor, depending on the setbacks your local authority requires. A ground-plus-one house gives more generous rooms.

Where should the car park go on a 30×50 plot?

Most often at the front of the ground floor, beside or in front of the entrance. Planning it together with the entrance keeps the living room from becoming a passage.

Do you design for east, west and south facing 30×50 plots too?

Yes. Every design starts from your plot's dimensions, facing and road, so each facing gets its own layout.
