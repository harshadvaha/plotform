Concept 01 · Kerala

Courtyard home, Kerala

  • 40 × 60 ft
  • East facing
  • Single storey
  • 3 bedrooms + pooja
  • About 1,110 sq ft built-up
Plan3D planElevation

2D ground floor plan, 40 × 60 ft, east facing, furnished. Open full size

Get this for your plot

The brief

Three generations under one roof. The grandparents want their room close to the pooja; everyone wants rain falling into the middle of the house and a breeze through every room.

How the design answers it

  • A nadumuttam (open courtyard) sits at the centre, so every room borrows light and air from it.
  • Clay-tile roofs slope in to the courtyard and out to deep eaves that keep the walls dry and shaded through the monsoon.
  • A poomukham sit-out with a timber gable faces the street, on a laterite plinth with a low laterite boundary wall.
  • Kitchen in the south-east, master bedroom in the south-west and pooja in the north-east, as Vastu asks.

Single storey. Plan, 3D plan and elevation show the whole house.

Concept 02 · Jaipur

Haveli-inspired home, Jaipur

  • 30 × 50 ft
  • North facing
  • Ground + 1
  • 4 bedrooms
  • About 1,540 sq ft built-up
Plan3D planElevation

2D ground floor plan, 30 × 50 ft, north facing, furnished. Open full size

Get this for your plot

The brief

A family that loves the old havelis of the walled city but wants a modern house that stays cool in 45°C summers, with a car porch, a baithak for guests and the parents on the ground floor.

How the design answers it

  • A double-height chowk with a skylight is the light well and gathering place, with a jhoola for the evenings.
  • Jharokha bays and sandstone jaali screens filter the street and the harsh sun on the front.
  • The baithak sits at the front with its own door, so guests never cross the family spaces.
  • A chhatri and a kangura parapet crown the terrace; kitchen in the south-east, parents in the south-west.

Ground floor shown in the plan and 3D plan. The upper floor holds three bedrooms around the open chowk.

Concept 03 · Ahmedabad

Vastu home with otla, Ahmedabad

  • 30 × 40 ft
  • East facing
  • Ground + 1
  • 3 bedrooms
  • About 1,170 sq ft built-up
Plan3D planElevation

2D ground floor plan, 30 × 40 ft, east facing, furnished. Open full size

Get this for your plot

The brief

A young couple living with parents. The elders want Vastu: an east entrance, the kitchen in the south-east, their room in the south-west. Everyone wants an otla with a hindola for evenings, and the car inside the gate.

How the design answers it

  • A raised otla runs across the front with a hindola, shaded by the balcony above.
  • Entrance in the north-east, kitchen in the south-east, parents in the south-west and the stair to the west.
  • Exposed brick on the street face and a brick jaali screening the balcony, in Ahmedabad’s brick-and-concrete tradition.
  • The car parks parallel to the road inside the front setback, leaving the otla clear.

Ground floor shown in the plan and 3D plan. The upper floor holds two bedrooms and the front balcony.

Concept 04 · Bengaluru

Compact rental home, Bengaluru

  • 20 × 30 ft
  • West facing
  • Ground + 2
  • Owner 1BHK + rental + office
  • About 960 sq ft built-up
Plan3D planElevation

2D ground floor plan, 20 × 30 ft, west facing, furnished. Open full size

Get this for your plot

The brief

A couple on a 600 sq ft plot: live on the ground floor, rent the first floor to help pay the EMI, and work from a quiet room on top with a terrace garden.

How the design answers it

  • A separate street door and staircase serve the upper floors, so tenants never pass through the owners’ home.
  • Timber louvres screen the stair column on the west face from the afternoon sun.
  • The top floor is a home office opening onto a terrace with a pergola and planters.
  • Solar panels and the water tank sit on the roof, out of sight from the street.

Ground floor (owners’ 1BHK) shown in the plan and 3D plan. First floor: rental 1BHK with balcony. Second floor: office and terrace.

The brief

A joint family that hosts often: a grand drawing room for gatherings, the elders’ bedroom on the ground floor, a puja room, a portico for two cars and a big front lawn for functions.
